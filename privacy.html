Privacy information

Staging draft, not a finalized public privacy policy. This page must be reviewed and approved by the app owner before a public launch. No final retention deadline or legal jurisdiction is asserted here.

Information used by the app

Scale & Tell uses an email-based Supabase account. The app can save keeper-entered animal profiles, care history, reminder settings, device and camera shortcuts, vet contacts, and journal photos. Private cloud records and photos are associated with the signed-in account.

How it is used

Information is used to provide the journal, restore records across sessions and devices, and display care schedules. If push reminders are later enabled, a device subscription and reminder preferences are needed to deliver opted-in alerts. Optional external links to cameras and community sites operate under those services' own terms.

Where it is stored

Account and cloud journal services use Supabase. The browser may also keep local app state or temporary copies; exported JSON and PDF files stay wherever the keeper saves or shares them. Do not share exports containing information you wish to keep private.

Your choices

Use in-app Data & Backup to export a copy. Use the in-app Privacy & Account section to prepare an account-deletion request, then send it privately to scaleandtellsupport@gmail.com. Preparing a request does not send it or instantly delete cloud data.

Prior to public release

App owner must confirm the final controller/contact identity, vendor disclosures, applicable legal notices, precise retention and deletion practice, any analytics or billing provider, effective date, and minors policy. This staging draft must be replaced by that approved text.
