Request account deletion

Staging procedure. Final identity verification, processing and retention commitments must be approved before public launch.

To request deletion of your Scale & Tell account and associated cloud keeper data, email scaleandtellsupport@gmail.com from your account email if possible. State that you are requesting account and associated cloud-record deletion. Do not send your password, private keys, or journal backup files.

Support must verify the request, review applicable retention requirements, and confirm the result. The in-app Prepare deletion request control composes text only and does not submit a request. Before changing accounts or clearing browser data, export a backup and verify its location if you need to retain it.
